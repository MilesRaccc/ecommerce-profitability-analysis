# E-commerce Profitability Analysis

Power BI analysis of e-commerce sales, profitability, returns, and marketing expenses.

## Business Problem

The company is experiencing declining profitability while marketing expenses are increasing.

The goal of the analysis is to identify the main factors affecting profitability and determine where further investigation is needed.

## Tools

- Power BI
- DAX

## Key Findings

### 1. Marketing expenses increased faster than revenue

Marketing expenses increased from €1,500 in June to €5,100 in August, while Gross Revenue increased from €2,400 to €2,900 and then remained flat.

This contributed to a significant decline in Net Profit over the same period.

### 2. Returns increased significantly

Return Rate increased from 0% in June to 20% in July and 40% in August. Returned Profit also increased, contributing to the decline in Net Profit.

## Recommendations

### 1. Conduct a deeper marketing analysis

Analyze campaign-level data such as marketing spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and attributed revenue to evaluate marketing efficiency and determine whether increasing expenses are justified by the results.

### 2. Investigate the increase in returns

Analyze return reasons, products, and categories to identify potential product quality or customer-experience issues contributing to the increase in returns.

## Limitations

- Marketing data is available only at the monthly level, without campaign-level performance metrics.
- The available marketing data does not include impressions, clicks, conversions, or attributed revenue, so campaign-level ROI and Conversion Rate cannot be evaluated.
- Return data identifies returned orders but does not provide detailed return reasons.
- The analysis covers a limited time period, so the findings should not be treated as long-term trends.
